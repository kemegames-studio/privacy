Privacy Policy

A Little Adventures · by Keme Games · Effective October 7, 2026

A Little Adventures is a racing game made for children. We built it so that children can play without sharing any personal information. This policy explains what happens with information when the game is played.

The short version

  • We do not ask for, collect or store names, email addresses, photos, contacts, location or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no way for players to contact each other.
  • Game progress (levels, cups, coins, cars and settings) is saved only on the device.
  • Ads are shown through Google AdMob with child-directed settings: no personalized ads and no advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play and are protected by a grown-up check.

Information stored on the device

The game saves its progress and settings in the app's local storage on the device. This data never leaves the device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows short ads only at the end of a level, never while driving. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (in line with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy) and limited to content rated G. The game does not use the Android advertising ID, and ads are not personalized or based on interests.

To deliver ads, measure that they were shown and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system and app version, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. Google handles this information as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

A one-time "No Ads" purchase removes all ads.

In-app purchases

Coins, cars and "No Ads" can be bought through Google Play Billing. Before any purchase, the game shows a grown-up check (a multiplication question) so that children cannot buy things by accident. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed, so the game can unlock it on the device.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check purchases with Google Play.
  • Vibration: for short, gentle vibrations during play. It can be turned off in the game's settings.

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and follows the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR's rules for children and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information about a child has been collected, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
